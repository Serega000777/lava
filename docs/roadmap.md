# Roadmap

1. Foundation: documentation, monorepo, infrastructure, health and categories.
2. Auth and profiles: sessions, OTP mock, password, VK adapter, roles and verification.
3. Listings: category attributes, drafts, media and lifecycle.
4. Moderation and complaints.
5. Search and discovery.
6. Favorites, messaging and notifications.
7. Reviews and reputation.
8. AI-assisted text and image cards with immutable credits.
9. Admin and seller analytics.
10. Security, accessibility, performance and production hardening.
